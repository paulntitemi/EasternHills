Why Kakum National Park Should Be Your Next Adventure

Kakum National Park, nestled in the Central Region of Ghana, is one of West Africa's most treasured rainforests. Spanning over 375 square kilometres of pristine tropical forest, it is home to a stunning variety of wildlife, rare plant species, and one of the continent's most famous attractions — the canopy walkway.

The park was established in 1931 as a forest reserve and later upgraded to a national park in 1992. Today, it attracts thousands of visitors each year, from adventure seekers walking 40 metres above the forest floor to birdwatchers hoping to spot some of the 266 bird species recorded within the park's boundaries.

The Canopy Walkway Experience

The highlight of any visit to Kakum is undoubtedly the canopy walkway — a series of seven suspension bridges stretching 350 metres through the treetops. Suspended 40 metres above the forest floor, the walkway offers breathtaking views of the forest canopy and a unique perspective on the ecosystem below. Early morning visits are recommended for the best wildlife sightings, as the forest comes alive with birdsong and the occasional glimpse of monkeys swinging through the branches. Our guides are trained naturalists who can identify species by their calls and help you spot creatures that most visitors would miss entirely.

Planning Your Visit

The best time to visit Kakum National Park is during the dry season, from November to March, when the trails are more accessible and wildlife viewing is at its peak. However, the park is beautiful year-round, and the rainy season brings its own charm with lush greenery and spectacular waterfalls. Eastern Hills EcoPark and Tour offers guided packages that include transportation from Accra or Cape Coast, park entrance fees, and experienced guides who bring the forest to life with their knowledge of local ecology and Akan cultural traditions connected to the forest. We also arrange visits to nearby Cape Coast Castle, making for a perfect combination of nature and history in a single trip.
